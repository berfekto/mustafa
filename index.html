About

me

Hi, I'm Mostafa — a passionate and certified swimming coach dedicated to helping swimmers grow both physically and mentally through a balanced and supportive training approach. I work with individuals of all ages and skill levels, focusing on developing technique, building strength, and building confidence in and out of the water. I have completed a wide range of professional certifications that reflect my commitment to safety, education, and performance, including: First Aid Training Courses Swimming Instructor Preparation Courses Stars Program Instructor Certification Basic Swimming Coaching Course Advanced Swimming Coaching Course Water Rescue and Lifesaving Certification What sets me apart is my calm, patient coaching style and my belief that every swimmer has their own unique potential. Coaching for me isn’t just about technique—it’s about trust, discipline, and creating a lasting love for the water. .
